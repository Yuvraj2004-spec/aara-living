# Showing your physical bedsheet designs

The reference patterns in the showroom are illustrative canvas prints. They are not scans of your products, and this photo-based preview does not accurately wrap a print around every fold. Reference mode cannot add a fictional design to the cart.

## Most accurate route: photograph the actual bed

Dress one bed with each real bedsheet and matching pillowcases. Keep the camera on a tripod, with the same framing, lighting and exposure for every design. Capture the complete bed, plus close-ups of the print, seams and fabric. Use the original high-resolution files, without beauty filters. These photographs show the real pattern, folds and placement together. Once supplied, each SKU can select its own photograph. Different bed sizes require their own photos if the preview must show their true appearance.

## Flexible route: capture a fabric swatch for a mapped preview

1. Lay the sheet flat and smooth in even, indirect daylight. Avoid mixed warm/cool lights, harsh shadows and reflections.
2. Photograph straight down, keeping the camera parallel to the fabric. Include at least one complete pattern repeat, ideally several. Use the original image rather than a compressed messaging-app copy. Include a ruler and a neutral colour reference in a separate calibration shot.
3. Record the physical width and height of one repeat in centimetres, print direction, actual sheet dimensions, pillowcase print and the product/SKU name. Send front/back details separately if different.
4. We crop, correct perspective and colour, and create a seamless tile where the design is repeating. Large placed motifs and borders need a full-sheet layout instead of tiling. Keep the original photo for comparison.
5. For convincing folds, apply the texture to a UV-mapped bedding mesh with measured scale, authored folds and material maps, or create an offline room render per design. The present screen-space canvas overlay is only a quick approximation and needs that upgrade for accurate print drape.
6. Compare the preview to your actual sheet before publishing. Keep real product photographs alongside the preview. Screens and room lighting can change colour appearance; do not promise exact physical colour matching.

Start by supplying one full-bed photo, one straight-on repeat photo and its repeat measurements. We can then establish the process before capturing the whole inventory. No database is required to associate local assets with catalogue IDs initially.
